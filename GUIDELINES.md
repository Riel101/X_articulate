## Curiating X posts thst go viral
Guidelines to follow when writing X post to guarantee virality.

### What must be achieved
-The hook must make the reader curious while the post spikes and/or satisfies curiousity.
-Must be easy to read, includes proper spacing and use of simpler words that are easier to understand.
-Must not be verbose, be concise.

Signal	How it influences recommendations
Reading time (dwell)	Predicts whether someone will spend meaningful time reading
Replies	Predicts whether someone will join the conversation
Reposts and shares	Predicts whether someone will distribute the post
Likes	Indicates potential interest
Profile visits and follows	Predicts interest in the author
Video watch time	Measures expected attention to videos
Blocks, mutes, reports	Negative signals that can reduce recommendations
Topic relevance	Helps match posts with interested audiences

01
Strong opening hook

Make the first sentence immediately relevant, surprising, or curiosity-inducing.


02
Reading retention

Deliver a compelling idea with clear structure so readers want to finish.


03
Meaningful discussion

Publish observations, useful disagreements, and questions that invite substantive replies.


04
Shareability

Offer information, insights, or humor that people want others to see.


05
Audience consistency

Build recognizable expertise around a few related topics instead of posting random viral trends.

**If any of the above is not achieved, iteratively finetune or rewrite till it is achieved.**

### Writing format
**Hook**: A creativily written one line sentence that instantly hooks the reader.
**Body**: The main contents of the post.
